# Energy-house-customer-analytics
Excel based customer segmentation and retention analysis for a simulated Nigerian e-commerce business.
Energy House — Customer Segmentation & Retention Analysis

Project Overview

This project explores customer purchasing behavior for Energy House, a simulated Nigerian e-commerce business.

The objective was to analyze customer segments, understand purchasing patterns, evaluate customer retention, and identify opportunities to improve customer loyalty and business performance.

The project was completed collaboratively as a team data analytics project using Microsoft Excel.

Business Problem

Energy House needs to understand its customers beyond total sales figures.

The analysis focuses on answering the following business questions:

* Which customer segments generate the most revenue and gross profit?
* How do new, returning, and loyal customers contribute to business performance?
* Which regions demonstrate stronger repeat-purchase patterns?
* How do acquisition channels relate to customer purchasing behavior?
* What strategies could help convert one-time buyers into repeat customers?

Dataset Overview

The dataset contains simulated Nigerian e-commerce transaction data.

Dataset Information	Details
Raw order records	1,812
Unique customers	569
Geographic coverage	10 regions
Acquisition channels	6
Data type	Simulated transaction data

The dataset includes customer identifiers, order information, acquisition channels, geographic regions, revenue, gross profit, customer ratings, and customer segments.

Project Workflow

1. Data Cleaning

The dataset was reviewed and prepared for analysis.

Key activities included:

* Identifying and removing duplicate order records.
* Reviewing missing values.
* Converting customer ratings from text to numeric values.
* Reviewing incomplete acquisition channel information.
* Preparing the dataset for customer analysis.

A detailed Data Cleaning Log is included in the workbook.

2. Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis explored:

* Revenue and gross profit by customer segment.
* Customer purchasing behavior.
* Repeat customer patterns.
* Average order value.
* Customer distribution across regions.
* Acquisition channel performance.

3. Customer Segmentation

Customers were analyzed using three segments:

* New: Customers making their first purchase.
* Returning: Customers who have made additional purchases.
* Loyal: Customers demonstrating stronger repeat-purchasing behavior.

4. Dashboard Development

An Excel dashboard was developed to present key customer and business performance metrics.

The dashboard provides a visual overview of customer behavior and supports data-driven decision-making.

Key Business Insights

The analysis examines customer revenue contribution, profitability, repeat purchasing, and retention patterns.

The project investigates:

* Which customer segments contribute most to revenue.
* Where repeat-purchase opportunities may exist.
* How customer behavior differs across regions and acquisition channels.
* Which customer metrics should be monitored to support retention strategies.

Detailed findings and supporting visualizations are available in the Excel workbook.

Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical objectives, potential business strategies include:

1. Developing targeted retention campaigns for customers with repeat-purchase potential.
2. Investigating regional differences in customer behavior.
3. Evaluating acquisition channels using customer quality and profitability, not just customer volume.
4. Monitoring repeat purchase rate, average order value, revenue, and gross profit.

These recommendations should be validated through further analysis and, where possible, controlled experiments.

Project Files

Project Limitations

* The dataset is simulated and does not represent verified transactions from a real business.
* The analysis identifies patterns and relationships but does not establish causation.
* Additional data, such as marketing costs and customer acquisition costs, would improve channel profitability analysis.
